Source: Zhao J, Peter D, Brandina I, Liu X, Galej WP. *Structural basis of 5′ splice site recognition by the minor spliceosome*. Molecular Cell (2025). [DOI:10.1016/j.molcel.2024.12.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2024.12.017), PMID 39809272.

All six are human isolated U11 particles or focused reconstructions, grouped under **U11 snRNP**. Focused entries are retained as separate deposited structures; they are not six distinct assembly states. Only 9GBW and 9GBZ contain a deposited substrate RNA entity, explicitly named P120-5′SS. 9GC0 comes from the substrate-bound preparation but its focused model contains no substrate RNA.

The existing atlas protein colors and systematic chain identities are reused. As in the existing minor-spliceosome convention, the U11 Sm ring uses U1-like systematic IDs; this does not assign a U1 RNA to these particles. U11 itself has systematic ID `11`. The supported U11 5′ recognition interval is reference-mapped and intersected with resolved residues. Approximate inherited stem-loop intervals were withheld pending paper-specific review. RNA 2D panels use deposited coordinates and R2DT/FR3D; absent coordinates do not establish biological absence.

[Curated inputs and source manifest](curation/zhao_2025_u11/source_manifest.json) record deposited metadata, map associations, component assignments, canonical chain mapping, and substrate/RNA decisions. These tables were processed in an isolated copy of the curation workspace with the maintained collectors, canonical-chain and ChimeraX generators, and public record builder. Only these six records and their assets were merged into the public snapshot; existing records were preserved. Full pipeline reproduction requires the curation archive described in DEVELOPMENT.md.

Thumbnails, RNA 2D panels, original-chain scripts, systematic-chain scripts, and both primary-map variants are bundled. Coordinates and maps are downloaded on demand; binaries are not committed. This update does not change the separate CBE/shared-filesystem dashboard or its payload.
