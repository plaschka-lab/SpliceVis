# Chlamydomonas C* spliceosome (8XI2)

Lu, Liang and Zhan, DOI [10.1038/s44318-024-00274-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44318-024-00274-3), PMID 39415054. Published online in 2024; EMBO Journal issue 44(4), 975–990 (2025). This is a cis C* spliceosome, 2.6 Å, with 29 protein copies (26 distinct protein entities) and five RNA chains (U2, U5, U6, intron, released 5′ exon). The paper counts the two substrate chains as one RNA element.

Protein assignments follow the visually reviewed Appendix Table S2, with Table S3, main text and peer-review response as context. Original deposition labels remain available. In particular, author chain C is Snu114, not chloroplastic EF-Tu; M is Syf2, not PPIL1 (S). Cwc21 shares the SRRM2/SRM300 family color for the conserved modeled N-terminal region; only residues 1–26 are modeled and full-length one-to-one orthology is not asserted. RBM22 is not split into separate yeast Cwc2/Ecm2 proteins. Every modeled protein has a paper-backed annotation and author accession.

EMD-38362 is the coordinate-associated Cdc5L-Flag map. EMD-38366 is the independent Prp19-Flag overall reconstruction, and EMD-38363/38364/38365/38367/38368 are focused maps. These are seven reconstructions supporting one coordinate model, available on demand.

Endogenous affinity purification does not establish a unique substrate transcript. Deposited substrate sequences are retained with this caveat. The 5′ exon is author residues −7 to −1; the intron has two resolved segments. No 3′ splice-site or 3′ exon assignment is invented. Algal snRNA annotations follow Figures 2B and 3 with algal numbering, including the short U6 5′ stem-loop, ACAGAGA motif and catalytic U67. The γ-monomethyl phosphate at U6 G1 is deposited as modified residue G5J.

The main article XML, appendix, expanded-view figures and peer-review PDF were archived and text-mined in the curation workspace. The algal model is not given a trans badge. Trans badges identify Leishmania 9IF7/9IF8 and share the minor-spliceosome badge style.
